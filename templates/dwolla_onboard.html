Setup guide

Dwolla does not embed a tutorial here. Follow these four steps. Sandbox is the default until DWOLLA_ENV=production.

1 · Company Legal name, EIN, address, and controller. This creates a Dwolla business customer. Customer docs
2 · Company bank Add the operating account Dwolla will deposit collections into, then verify it. Funding source docs
3 · First bank link On a borrower, save routing and account and check ACH authorized (or email the ACH form from the loan). The first collect creates that person’s Dwolla customer and bank.
4 · First ACH Open the loan, save the ACH plan, and choose Collect this payment now. When Dwolla reports the transfer complete, the loan ledger posts automatically. Point Dwolla webhooks at /webhooks/dwolla. Webhook docs
