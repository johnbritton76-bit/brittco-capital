Setup steps

1 · Sandbox vs Production Create a Dwolla account at sandbox first. Switch to a production Dwolla account only after sandbox ACH works. Set DWOLLA_ENV=sandbox or DWOLLA_ENV=production to match.
2 · Application Key / Secret In the Dwolla Dashboard open Applications → create an application → copy the Key and Secret. Those become DWOLLA_KEY and DWOLLA_SECRET on the host. Do not commit them.
3 · Webhook In Dwolla, create a webhook subscription pointing at {{ webhook_host_hint }}/webhooks/dwolla (replace the host with your live app URL). Subscribe at least to transfer_completed, transfer_failed, and transfer_cancelled. Copy the webhook secret into DWOLLA_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Webhook docs
4 · Host environment variables On Render (or your host), set: DWOLLA_KEY, DWOLLA_SECRET, DWOLLA_ENV, DWOLLA_WEBHOOK_SECRET, and PLATFORM_MODE. Optional: DWOLLA_MASTER_CUSTOMER_URL (and DWOLLA_PLATFORM_FUNDING_SOURCE_URL) when the business customer already exists and you want to skip re-creating it. See .env.example in the repo — values stay out of git.
5 · Business Verified Customer + funding source {% if platform_mode == 'whitelabel' %} Open Company ACH setup and create the Business Verified Customer, then add and verify the operating bank (funding source). {% else %} Paste the existing customer URL as DWOLLA_MASTER_CUSTOMER_URL, or add the company bank on the ACH page once the customer URL is set. {% endif %} Sandbox bank routing 222222226; micro-deposits 0.01 and 0.02 verify the account.
6 · First test ACH On a borrower, save routing/account and check ACH authorized (or send the ACH form). Open a loan, save the ACH plan, and choose Collect this payment now. When Dwolla sends transfer_completed, the loan ledger posts automatically. Confirm the event on the ACH log.
